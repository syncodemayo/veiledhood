VEILEDHOOD — BRAND ASSETS
=========================

THE MARK
The Arch x Aperture: one arch band with a nested core inside its void.
Reads as hood at a glance, closing aperture on second read.

Artboard note: the mark's ink spans 72 of 100 viewBox units — there are
14 units of clear padding on each side, built in. Do not add padding on
top of it when placing in a component.


COLOUR
Electric violet   #8257FF   primary, use on dark and on light
Violet lift       #A88CFF   the mark and "Hood" on dark grounds
Violet deep       #5B32D6   gradient end only
Ink               #08090B   dark ground
Paper             #F4F4F6   light ground


TYPE
Archivo    wordmark and display. "Veiled" 500, "Hood" 800.
DM Mono    labels, micro-type, data.

Wordmark is set in Archivo, so wordmark assets ship as PNG only.
The mark itself ships as SVG — always prefer the SVG.


CLEAR SPACE
x = the span of the mark's open void, measured between the band's
inner edges (50 of 100 viewBox units = half the artboard width).
Nothing enters that margin: no type, no rule, no other logo.

COMPACT MARK
Below 20px the nested core closes up, so the compact mark drops it and
keeps the band alone. Use compact for favicons, token lists and any slot
at or under 20px. Use the full mark everywhere else.


DO NOT
- Do not add padding around the mark; it is already built in.
- Do not recolour the mark outside the palette above.
- Do not set the wordmark in any face other than Archivo.
- Do not place the wordmark on the violet ground in violet; knock it out to white.
